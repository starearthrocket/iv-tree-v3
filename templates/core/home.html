How it works

Report. Protect. Monitor.

Every report helps build a clearer picture of trees affected by invasive ivy and the work being done to protect them.

Report

Record an affected tree with its location, description and photograph.

02

Protect

Share useful information and record action taken to help protect the tree.

03

Monitor

Add progress updates over time and build a visible history of the tree's condition.
